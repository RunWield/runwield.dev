# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Experienced developers evaluating AI-assisted work on real, existing codebases, as described in the current site and RunWield README.

## Product Purpose

RunWield connects reviewed Plans, deliberate execution, validation, and durable Work Records. The public site helps a developer understand the workflow and try it on a real change.

## Positioning

Lead the Features page with the learning flywheel (owner confirmed 2026-10-06): recorded lessons and decisions inform future planning. Do not imply automatic model training or proven continuous improvement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The detailed inventory lives in the sibling RunWield repository at `docs/user-facing-features.md`. Verify maturity against implementation and delivery records; central PRDs distinguish current and target behavior. Connect, reusable factory components, broader team governance, and metrics dashboard/export are future work. Image generation through create_image works with OpenRouter, Codex, and Antigravity CLI (owner confirmed 2026-10-06). Pi is an implementation library, not the user-facing backend name.

## Brand Commitments

In visible titles and headings, always render “Run” in white (`--rw-text-strong`) and “Wield” in mint (`--rw-logo`). Keep the name joined as RunWield.

Keep the current RunWield visual style. The Features page uses high-level categories and expandable detail, inspired by the structure at https://voidleap.com/features/.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing Plan Review and Code Review screenshots are in `public/images/`; the home page includes the demo. Do not invent usage statistics or customer evidence.

## External clients

Owner confirmed 2026-10-06: ACP works with JetBrains AI Chat and the Air plugin; OpenAB works end to end through Discord and its other supported chat platforms. Other ACP-compatible clients should work too. Present these user outcomes in feature copy, not internal protocol test checklists or stale rollout gates.

## Feature copy

Describe what users can do and why it helps them decide to try RunWield. Keep test status, proof caveats, sandbox limitations, and implementation machinery out of marketing copy. When current feature availability is unclear, ask the owner instead of inferring an unavailable or future state from old documentation. Preserve useful configuration choices and actual product constraints in the relevant technical documentation.
